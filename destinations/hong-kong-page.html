HONG KONG

Victoria Peak

Victoria Peak is the highest point on Hong Kong Island, making it one of Hong Kong's most iconic viewpoint. Offering sweeping skyline views, and a peaceful escape above the busy city.

Causeway Bay

Causeway Bay is a vibrant hub for shopping, dining and bright city energy. It embodies the fast-paced spirit of Hong Kong, with its trendy stores and local eats.

Ocean Park

Ocean Park Hong Kong blends thrilling rides, marine life and scenic coastal views, making it a fun-filled destination for both adventure seekers and families.
