TAIWAN

Taipei

Taipen is a lively capital where modern skyscrapers, night markets, and rich culture come together, offering everything from iconic landmarks to delicious streed food.

Jiufen

Jiufen is a charming hillside village known for narrow lantern-lit streets, tea houses and nostalgic views overlooking the sea.

Taroko Gorge

Taroko Gorge is a breathtaking natural wonder of towering marble cliffs, winding trails and rushing rivers, perfect for exploring Taiwan's dramatic landscapes.
